THE WORTHY ONE - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Contact: mrcarkstudios@gmail.com

THE WORTHY ONE ("the game") is a mobile game. You can play it entirely offline without an account. This policy explains what is

stored, where, and how to delete it.

What the game stores on your phone

  • Your best level and time in each mode, settings (volume, quality, frame rate) and whether you chose to play without an account. This stays on your phone. Uninstalling the game removes it.

What is sent to our server (only if you create an account or play online)

The online features use a hosted database service (Supabase). If you log in or create an account, the game sends and stores:

  • Username (you choose it; it must be unique and is shown in the Hall of Fame).
  • Password, which is stored only as a one-way hash. We cannot read it and cannot recover it. There is no password reset.
  • Game results: which mode you played, the levels you completed and how long each took, and when a run ended. This is used to build the Hall of Fame (rankings) and to check results for cheating.
  • An anonymous session identifier that the hosting service creates for each install, so your phone can stay logged in.

Your username, level and time are visible to other players in the Hall of Fame.

A short line of text after a run

After a run the game may ask a server function for one line of flavour text. The request contains only the mode, the level reached

and how the run ended. It contains no username and no personal information. The text may be generated by an AI service.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, do not sell data, do not collect your location, contacts, photos or device identifiers for advertising, and do not

use analytics or tracking services.

How long we keep data

Account data is kept while the account exists. Accounts that have not been used for 90 days may be removed.

Deleting your account and data

  • In the game: open Account, choose Delete account, and type your password. Your username, records and runs are removed from our server and the name becomes free again. This cannot be undone.
  • Without the game or if you cannot log in: write to mrcarkstudios@gmail.com with your username and we will delete the account. Because there is no email on the account, we may ask you to describe your best level and time to confirm it is yours.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Permissions

The game asks for Internet access (online accounts and the Hall of Fame) and vibration (a short buzz when you touch a button). It does not

ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version is published at the same address.
