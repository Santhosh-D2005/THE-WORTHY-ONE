THE WORTHY ONE - Delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your account for the Android game THE WORTHY ONE (package com.theworthyone.game).

Delete it inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open THE WORTHY ONE and log in.
  2. On the main menu, tap ACCOUNT.
  3. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT and type your password.
  4. Tap DELETE FOREVER.

Your account is deleted at once. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot log in or no longer have the game

Email mrcarkstudios@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my THE WORTHY ONE account" and your username. Because accounts have no email address, we may ask you to describe your best level and time to confirm the account is yours. We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your username and password hash.
  • Your Hall of Fame records, your runs and level times, and your session data.

The username becomes free for anyone to register again. Progress stored only on your own phone is removed when you uninstall the game.

See also the Privacy Policy.
